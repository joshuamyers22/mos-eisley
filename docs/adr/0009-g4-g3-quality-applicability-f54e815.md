## Context and options

The G4 delivery contract requires any **applicable** G3 quality gate before G4
acceptance. G3's empirical study compares context policies, model routes and
cost or savings claims across representative, independently graded cases. Its
contracts require a sealed design, eligible independent labels, a protected
holdout and whole-task outcomes. A single passing task is not that study.

This G4 run is an isolated qualification of the **correction-child path** for
the approved bounded-quote task. The approved task plan explicitly excludes a
real initial-child exercise from this claim. This run used separately approved
fixed provider/model calls and full two-family critic plus judge coverage. It
does not activate a context selector, automatic model routing, reduced review,
or an optimized policy; it makes no comparative utility or savings claim.

The exact integrated commit passed both protected creator and frozen reviewer
whole suites. The signed single-operator two-provider review accepted that
commit. ADR-0008 and its exact owner signature permit that review to satisfy
the formal implementation-review requirement for this commit while preserving
the original independent-human-review failure. The failed-call spending holds
were separately reconciled with a signed owner decision; historical audit
records remain visible.

## Decision and consequences

For **this exact, narrow correction-path qualification claim**, the G3
comparative empirical study is **not applicable**. The applicable quality
evidence is the replayed G4 provenance, passing final whole suites, accepted
two-provider review, and signed ADR-0008 exception. Joshua must sign a separate
domain-separated applicability decision binding those exact artifacts and this
ADR before this determination is effective.

This decision does **not** report a G3 study pass or independently graded
representative utility. It cannot support context-selection, routing, review
reduction, savings, cross-task quality, full initial-child workflow, or
production activation claims. Any such claim requires the applicable G3
pre-registered study, real eligible labels, protected holdout, independent
grading and statistical review. Another G4 commit or task needs its own
applicability decision. G4 acceptance still requires Joshua's distinct final
decision; this ADR grants no acceptance, merge, release or provider authority.
