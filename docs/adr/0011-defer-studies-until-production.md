# ADR-0011: Defer all studies until Mos Eisley is in production

- Status: accepted owner direction; no study is authorized
- Date and owner: 2026-09-28, Joshua Myers

## Context and options

The earlier G3 plan allowed an offline utility study and a separate narrow
objective-oracle study before product launch. The continuous production plan
already requires real outcomes of owner-authorized tasks for production
effectiveness, harm and savings decisions. Joshua has now directed that **no
studies will be done until Mos Eisley is in production**. Keeping the earlier
preproduction study path would conflict with that direction.

The choice is to retain preproduction empirical studies or defer every study
until an explicit product production launch. This decision adopts the latter.

## Decision and consequences

From this decision forward, no study begins or continues before Mos Eisley is
in production. G3, G5, the narrow objective-oracle proposal and the continuous
production program remain design and engineering work only.
Protocol drafting, source and rights audits, fixture tests, oracle controls,
feasibility calculations, and launch qualification may continue. They create no
study cohort, enrolled case, signed study label, arm assignment, holdout use,
outcome-bearing comparison, efficacy estimate or production claim. Existing
public-source audits and earlier engineering experiments retain their historical
status; neither authorizes new study execution.

Study execution requires a documented product production launch, actual
operation on real owner-authorized tasks, **and** the applicable prospective
source, method, governance, sample, spend, safety and outcome gates. A paper
launch decision alone authorizes no study. Real owner-authorized task
outcomes are the acceptance evidence for production effectiveness, harm and
savings. An objective-oracle study, if separately approved after launch, can
make only its audited source-bound claim and cannot substitute for those real
outcomes.

This scheduling decision waives no product release or capability gate. Any
capability that depends on G3 or G5 comparative evidence stays disabled until
its postlaunch study qualifies it. Product engineering and release review may
proceed under their other applicable gates without treating a planned study as
completed evidence. The owner can change this timing only through an explicit
new decision before any study activity.

## Verification

Before admitting the first study task, verify the exact production launch record,
evidence of actual ordinary production operation and a separately approved,
externally timestamped protocol; check that the source, labels, groups,
probabilities, splits, arms, resource ceiling and outcome custody meet that
protocol. Reject prelaunch study assignments or claims and any
attempt to infer missing study metadata. Report public benchmark and fixture
results as engineering diagnostics regardless of where they are run. Reconsider
this decision only on a later explicit owner direction.
