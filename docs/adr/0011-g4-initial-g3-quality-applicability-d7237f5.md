acceptance. G3's empirical study compares context policies, model routes and
cost or savings claims across independently graded, representative tasks. It
requires an eligible label inventory, sealed arms, a protected holdout and
whole-task outcomes. One passing coding task cannot establish that study.

## Decision and consequences

For this exact, narrow **initial-child path qualification claim**, the G3
comparative empirical study is **not applicable**. The applicable task-quality
evidence is the replayed G4 initial-child provenance, passing final whole
suites, accepted two-provider review and signed one-human formal exception.
Joshua must separately sign a domain-separated decision binding the approved
plan, source commit, child and final-suite receipts, review subject and record,
formal exception, spend disposition and this ADR before the determination is
effective.

This decision does not report a G3 study pass, representative quality or
savings, context-policy benefit, route optimization, reduced-review safety,
or independently graded whole-task utility. It qualifies neither the combined
creator-led G4 workflow nor production activation. Those broader claims need
their own applicable G3 study and separate authority. Another source commit or
task needs another applicability decision. G4 creator acceptance, merge, push,
release, and provider use remain separate gates.
