# G4 review of the integrated real coding child

This route reconstructs a review subject from the exact passing carry-forward
final-suite receipt, signed integration, renewed policy, original creator-approved
plan, and complete base-to-integrated Git diff. Its lineage record binds the
historical authenticated provenance, signed integration, renewed policy, new
implementation binding, passing candidate and final-suite receipt. The lineage
hash is an adapter identity; it does not claim a new historical E2 attestation.

The subject and compatibility context are produced only after the complete
carry-forward final-suite receipt replays against current clean Git and private
claims. Existing single-operator review contracts can then bind a fresh owner
signature, OpenAI and Anthropic critic observations, a derived judge request, and
a separate owner decision to this new subject. Existing critic and judge live
brokers retain their one-use grant, no-retry, private audit, and conservative
shared-ledger behavior. Every call needs a newly signed exact transfer and spend
grant; the review authority alone cannot make a provider call.

The final record is one-owner review evidence and may adjudicate to `accept`,
`revise` or `reject`. Its result does not prove independent human judgment or
authorize implementation acceptance, release, Git merge, or another provider
call. The [formal independent-review gate](G4_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW.md) continues
to require distinct external critic and judge signers.
